How It Works

Data Processing

We process 5000+ movies from the TMDB dataset, extracting genres, keywords, cast, and crew information.

02
🧬

Vectorization

Movie metadata is converted into numerical vectors using Bag of Words (CountVectorizer) with stemming.

03
🔗

Cosine Similarity

We compute pairwise similarity between all movies to find the closest content matches.

04
🎯

Top 5 Picks

The 5 most similar movies are returned, ranked by their cosine similarity score.
